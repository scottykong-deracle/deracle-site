Resistive Touch Technology

4-wire and 5-wire resistive touch configurations.

4-Wire Resistive

X and Y voltage gradients are applied across the two conductive layers.

5-Wire Resistive

The bottom conductive layer provides the voltage gradient, while the top layer senses touch.

Optical Bonding Structure

OCA Bonding

Optically clear adhesive film used for film-based bonding applications.

OCR Bonding

Optically clear resin applied as a liquid bonding material and cured as required.

Process configuration depends on the display structure, bonding material and manufacturing partner.
