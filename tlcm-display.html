Overview

DERACLE has launched its Touch LCD Complete Module (TLCM), integrating a premium IPS LCD panel, projected capacitive touch screen and Optical Clear Adhesive (OCA) bonding into one industrial-ready display solution.


Designed for Gaming, Industrial, Medical, POS, Kiosk and HMI applications requiring high reliability, improved visibility and long-term product availability.


Additional TLCM models will be continuously updated.

Optical Clear Adhesive (OCA) Bonding Process

OCA bonding joins the touch assembly and LCD panel with optically clear adhesive to reduce the air gap and help improve display readability.

1

Component Preparation

Prepare and inspect the cover glass, touch sensor, adhesive film and LCD panel.

2

OCA Film Lamination

Position the optically clear adhesive between the relevant optical layers.

3

Vacuum Bonding

Bond the assembly using the supplier’s controlled process to minimize trapped air.

4

Finished TLCM

Inspect the completed touch display module before integration.

Benefits of OCA Bonding

  • Reduced internal reflections
  • Improved display readability
  • Clear optical appearance
  • Integrated touch and display assembly
  • Suitable for industrial and kiosk projects
