Frequently Asked Questions

+ What is the difference between SD Card and microSD Card?

Both products provide the same storage functionality. The primary difference is physical size. microSD Cards are designed for compact embedded devices, while SD Cards are commonly used in larger industrial equipment and portable devices.

+ Do you offer Industrial Grade Storage Cards?

Yes. DERACLE supplies both commercial and industrial grade Storage Card solutions with stable supply for embedded and industrial applications.

+ What capacities are available?

Capacity options typically range from 8GB to 512GB depending on product type, application requirements and availability.

+ Can DERACLE recommend the right Storage Card?

Yes. Our engineering team can recommend the appropriate SD Card, microSD Card or USB Flash Drive based on interface, capacity, operating environment and product lifecycle requirements.
