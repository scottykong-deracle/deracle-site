Frequently Asked Questions

+ What is the difference between SD Card and microSD Card?

SD Card and microSD Card provide the same storage functionality. The primary difference is physical size. microSD Cards are designed for compact embedded devices, while SD Cards are commonly used in industrial equipment, cameras and portable systems.

+ What is CFexpress Card?

CFexpress is a next-generation removable storage technology based on PCIe and NVMe interfaces. It delivers significantly higher read and write performance than conventional SD Cards and is widely used in professional cameras, industrial imaging and high-speed recording applications.

+ Do you provide Industrial Grade Storage Cards?

Yes. DERACLE supplies both commercial and industrial grade storage products with stable supply, dependable quality and engineering support for long-life embedded systems.

+ What capacities are available?

Available capacities vary by product family. SD Card, microSD Card and USB Flash Drive products are available from 8GB up to 1TB, while CFexpress Cards are available up to 2TB depending on the selected series.

+ Can DERACLE recommend the most suitable storage solution?

Yes. Our engineering team can recommend the appropriate storage product based on interface, capacity, operating environment, application requirements and long-term product lifecycle.
